Privacy Policy

How Bloomora collects, uses, and protects your personal information. Last updated: January 2026.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly — such as your name, email address, phone number, delivery address, and payment details when you place an order, subscribe to our newsletter, or contact our team.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to process and deliver orders, send order updates, respond to enquiries, provide flower subscriptions, and — only with your consent — send seasonal offers and tips.

Information Sharing

We never sell your personal information. We only share the minimum data required with trusted partners who help us operate, such as delivery carriers and payment processors, all bound by confidentiality agreements.

Cookies

Our site uses essential cookies for functionality — such as remembering your preferred theme — and analytics cookies to understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it.

Data Security

We use industry-standard safeguards to protect your data in transit and at rest. Payment information is processed by certified, PCI-compliant payment providers and is never stored on our servers.

Your Rights

You may access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time. You can also unsubscribe from marketing emails with a single click or by contacting us directly.
