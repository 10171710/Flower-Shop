We make everyday moments bloom

Bloomora is a floral studio built on craft, colour and care.‎ Since 2012 we've designed tens of thousands of arrangements for homes, weddings and businesses.‎

Our Mission

To make beautiful, ethically-sourced flowers effortless — delivered fresh, designed with intention, and priced fairly for every occasion.

Our Vision

A world where every home, workplace and celebration carries the warmth of fresh seasonal flowers — without waste, without compromise.

Our Promise

A 7-day bloom guarantee on every arrangement, plastic-free packaging, and flowers that are always locally and responsibly grown.

Our story

From a market stall to a metro studio

What began as a weekend flower stall in 2012 grew into a full-service studio with our own refrigerated delivery fleet — but the hands behind every bouquet are still ours.

2012

The first stall

Founder Iris Bennett sells her first 40 bouquets at the riverside market.

2016

Our first studio

A storefront on Rosewood Avenue with a walk-in cooler and a team of four.

2020

Same-day delivery

Our own delivery fleet launches with a live-tracking app and 2 PM cutoff.

2026

Zero-waste studio

100% plastic-free packaging and composting partners across the city.
